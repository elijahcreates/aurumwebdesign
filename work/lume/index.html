Our story

One fire, one room.

Everything on the menu passes through the same wood-fired hearth, from the bread at the start of the night to the fruit at the end of it.

We keep the menu short so we can cook it properly, and we change it when the season does. The room seats forty, the kitchen is open, and nobody will rush you.
