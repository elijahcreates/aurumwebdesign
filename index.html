05Good Questions

Everything you want to know first

How much does a website cost?

Websites start at $229. The final price depends on how many pages and features you need. Message us on WhatsApp and you’ll get a free quote before any work begins.

How long does it take?

Most sites are ready in 2–4 business days. Larger sites can take longer, and you’ll get a clear timeline together with your quote.

What do you need from me to get started?

A logo, a few photos and a short description of your business are a great start. If you don’t have those yet, we’ll help you work them out.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed for phones first, then tablets and computers, so it looks right on any screen.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The website and everything on it are yours.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes. We can give an existing site a full redesign so it matches the quality of your business.
